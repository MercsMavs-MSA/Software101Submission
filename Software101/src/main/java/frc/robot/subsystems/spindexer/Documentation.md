In Software 101, I learned about how to PID tune motors and how that fits in with feedForward values to get a motor to reach the specific position or velocity you need to. There are 4 feedForward values kG, kP, kV, and kS to provide the initial “voltage proportion” to the motor to a specific point and the PID motors are used to help adjust the error. I also learned about motor controllers and how an entire motor unit is the motor, controller (TalonFX), and the encoder. I also learned about graph values and status signals. I’m going to use this in the future to help me understand how to run the robot, navigate the driver station, and also potentially diagnose problems with motors, voltage, and other potential issues that can be found with Phoenix Tuner. I’ll also use it to help me understand why teams made certain decisions on a high level for their software and why the smaller things matter as well in terms of like logs and the amount of information that is sent over a CANbus.
